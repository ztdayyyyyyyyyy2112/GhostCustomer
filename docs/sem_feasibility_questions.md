# SEM feasibility and lecturer clarification

The course outline names structural equation modeling (SEM), while the current research plan uses behavioral event logs and does not specify survey-based latent constructs. The project should not promise SEM estimates until a defensible measurement model and suitable variables are established.

## Questions to resolve

1. Is SEM mandatory for every project, or may a group document why it is unsuitable for its data and answer the research questions with regression and machine learning instead?
2. If SEM is mandatory, are observed behavioral indicators and a fully specified measurement/structural model acceptable, or does the course expect survey items for latent constructs?
3. What minimum reporting is expected: identification, fit indices, reliability/validity, effect estimates, and assumptions?
4. Would a descriptive path model using event-derived variables count, and what cautions about causal language should accompany it?

## Proposed decision path

- Record the lecturer's response, date, and exact conditions in D-07 of `docs/decision_log.md`.
- If valid constructs and adequate data exist, pre-specify the measurement model and assess sample size, missingness, identification, and fit before reporting paths.
- If not feasible, write an explicit limitation in the report and use only analyses supported by the event data. Do not fabricate survey responses, latent scores, fit indices, or significance values.

This document is a draft for the team to discuss with the lecturer. It does not claim a waiver or approval.
