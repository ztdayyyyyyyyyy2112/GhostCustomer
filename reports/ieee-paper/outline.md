# IEEE paper writing outline

Target: 7–10 pages in two-column IEEE conference style. Produce the final paper after the analysis has reproducible results. Use the same cohort, window, label, dates, estimates, figures, and model version as the full report. Draft directly from verified source tables and figures; do not copy-paste unverified numbers from slides or the dashboard.

1. **Title, abstract, keywords.** Write the abstract last: problem, data, cohort size, method, principal numerical results, limitation, and contribution. Do not include a performance claim before held-out evaluation.
2. **Introduction.** Define the operational warning-stage problem and four research questions. Explain why event time boundaries matter.
3. **Related work.** Compare churn/disengagement definitions, behavioral decay, temporal validation, inference, and forecasting. Cite original research and dataset provenance.
4. **Data and methodology.** Show the eligible-cohort flow, pre-T feature construction, future confirmation label, unknown handling, temporal split, statistical tests, regression, forecast, baselines, and ML evaluation. Report SEM status based on lecturer guidance and actual measurement data.
5. **Results.** Reserve concise tables for cohort counts, group summaries/tests, regression coefficients, forecasting comparison, and model comparison. Include selected figures with legible axes, units, N, and captions. Every value must have a reproducible source.
6. **Discussion.** Answer RQ1–RQ4, distinguish association from causation, discuss class imbalance, right-censoring, sample selection, short time series, and implications for retention decisions.
7. **Conclusion and future work.** Summarize supported findings and the next validation needed before operational use.
8. **References.** Use IEEE citation order and verify all bibliographic fields and links.

Before submission, compare every number and figure caption against the full report and saved result files. Check page count, two-column layout, references, plagiarism/AI-audit evidence, and direct upload to the course system.
